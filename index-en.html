Profile

I am an enterprise risk consultant at a global risk advisory and insurance broking firm. Before that I spent five years at Deloitte as a senior internal auditor, where I ran more than ten internal audits and led control testing under the Sarbanes-Oxley Act. Today I design risk methodologies, policies and key risk indicators for companies across sectors and sizes, and I facilitate the workshops where those risks are discussed with executive committees.

The tools on this page came out of that work: I built them because I needed them.

Interactive demos

The instruments I work with

Each one solves a problem I have run into in the field: valuing and prioritising, mapping causes and barriers, reconciling mismatched matrices, setting appetite thresholds and deciding how much to test. They are live — open them and use them. The interfaces are in Spanish; the frameworks behind them are COSO and ISO 31000.

These are demonstration versions. They are deliberately simplified and carry sample data: they exist to show the approach in five minutes, not to run a risk function on. The full versions are configured for each organisation’s methodology, rating scales and reporting. If one of them is genuinely useful to you, write to me and I will show you the working version.

Process Risk Matrix

Training and risk culture

Mini-games for courses and workshops

Risk culture is not built with slides. For courses and workshops I designed short exercises styled as 8-bit video games: participants make decisions, get the explanation straight away and see the result of their judgement take shape. Each takes about five minutes, alone or as a group with the exercise on a projector.

These are beta versions. They are complete, but still being tested with groups and may change. They are in Spanish. If you would like to use them in a course or workshop at your organisation, write to me and we will adapt them to your risks.
