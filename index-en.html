Training and risk culture

Mini-games for courses and workshops

Risk culture is not built with slides. For courses and workshops I designed short exercises styled as 8-bit video games: participants decide, get the explanation straight away and see the result of their judgement take shape. Each takes about five minutes, alone or as a group with the exercise on a projector.

These are beta versions: complete, but still being tested with groups. They are in Spanish. If you would like to use them in a course or workshop at your organisation, write to me and we will adapt them to your risks.

Profile

A career in risk and control

I am an enterprise risk consultant at Marsh, a global risk advisory and insurance broking firm. I design risk management systems — policy, methodology and key risk indicators — for companies across sectors and sizes, and I facilitate the workshops where those risks are discussed with executive committees. My experience includes five years as a senior internal auditor at Deloitte, with more than ten internal audits carried out and leadership of control testing under the Sarbanes-Oxley Act.
