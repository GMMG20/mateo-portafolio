Risk management, in working order

“Risk that gets managed, not documented. These are the instruments I do it with.”

I am an enterprise risk consultant at Marsh. Before that I spent five years at Deloitte as a senior internal auditor, where I ran more than ten internal audits and led control testing under the Sarbanes-Oxley Act. Today I design risk methodologies, policies and key risk indicators for companies across sectors and sizes, and I facilitate the workshops where those risks get discussed with executive committees.

The tools on this page came out of that work: I built them because I needed them.

The instruments I work with

Each one solves a problem I have run into in the field: valuing and prioritising, mapping causes and barriers, reconciling mismatched matrices, setting appetite thresholds and deciding how much to test. They are live — open them and use them. The interfaces are in Spanish; the frameworks behind them are COSO and ISO 31000.

These are demonstration versions. They are deliberately simplified and carry sample data: they exist to show the approach in five minutes, not to run on. The full versions are configured to each organisation’s methodology, rating scales and reporting. If one of them is genuinely useful to you, write to me and I will show you the working version.
